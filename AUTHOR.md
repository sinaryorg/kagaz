## 🚀 The Story Behind Kagaz

As a developer and notes enthusiast, I wanted a modern knowledge management desktop application that was:
- **⚡ Fast and Lightweight**: Built with Electron, Vite, React 19, and TypeScript for peak performance.
- **🎨 Clean & Minimalist**: A distraction-free workspace tailored for notes, markdown, and graph linking.
- **📂 Full Data Ownership**: Pure markdown files stored locally on your own system.

Kagaz was designed from the ground up to give users absolute control over their knowledge base while maintaining high visual elegance.
